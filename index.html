01 / Mission briefing

Learn what it takes to live beyond Earth.

ASTRION turns the challenges of operating a lunar habitat into a hands-on educational experience. Players take on astronaut responsibilities through mission objectives, equipment interactions and a structured 14-quest progression.

✦
Not just exploration — outpost operations.

ASTRION focuses on what happens after arrival: supporting the crew, managing essential resources, maintaining habitat systems and responding to mission challenges. It is an educational Minecraft simulation, not certified astronaut training or a scientifically validated operational simulator.

Optional visual enhancement

ASTRION shader archive: Optional client-side shader pack for players who want enhanced lighting and atmosphere. It is not required to play ASTRION. Follow the installation notes included in the archive and check compatibility with your Minecraft version and shader loader.

↓ Download Shader Pack ZIP

Vanilla experience: ASTRION does not require a custom resource pack. Original Minecraft textures, blocks, items and user interface are preserved. The shader is optional.

World installation: Download the edition you want, extract the ZIP, then move the extracted world folder into your Minecraft Java saves folder. Keep the extracted folder name intact. Do not place the ZIP itself in saves. Back up existing worlds before installing.

Shader installation: Use the instructions in the included HOW TO INSTALL.txt. Shader installation is optional and does not change the world files.
